How it works

From recording to triaged kanban in under a minute.

Four steps. No training the model on your team's slang. No "set up your workspace" wizard. Just drop and go.

1

Record or upload

Browser recorder, Mac/iOS app, or paste an .m4a / .mp3 / .wav. Drag-drop or hit Cmd-Shift-R.

2

AI transcribes and extracts

Whisper transcribes, GPT identifies tasks, owners, due dates, and blockers. Each task cites its transcript span.

3

Triage in the inspector

Tasks land in Inbox. Open the inspector, fix anything the AI got wrong, then push to a project. Source span is one click away.

4

Work the board (or list)

Switch between Kanban for triage and Tasks list for power use. Filter by owner, group by project. Cmd-K everywhere.

Getting started

Two minutes from signup to first extracted task.

Auth runs through Zitadel SSO so we never see your password. Pick email + password or a passkey. Either works in under 30 seconds.

Step 01

Click Get started

You land on Zitadel's hosted register screen. Email + password, or "Add passkey" with Face ID, Touch ID, or Windows Hello. No magic links, no email confirmation gauntlet.

Step 02

Drop a meeting recording

Inside the app, hit Cmd-Shift-R to record in-browser, or upload an .m4a / .mp3 / .wav. The first file does not need any setup or project to be created.

FAQ

Questions worth answering directly.

How does signup work? I do not see a signup form.
Auth runs through Zitadel, an open-source identity provider. Click Get started, you land on Zitadel's hosted page, register with email + password or a passkey, and you are back in kAInban in under 30 seconds. We never see or store your password.
How accurate is the transcription?
We use Azure OpenAI Whisper. Real-world accuracy is ~95% on clean audio, lower on noisy or accented speech. Every transcript is editable, and tasks cite their span so you can verify before you trust.
Can I self-host?
Yes. The full source is AGPL-3.0 on GitHub. Docker compose in the repo handles the API, frontend, and Zitadel. You bring your own OpenAI / Azure key. Managed instance at app.kainban.com is for people who would rather not run ops.
What happens to my recordings?
Recordings stay in your workspace storage (your filesystem if self-hosted, encrypted at rest if managed). They are sent to Whisper for transcription only; the OpenAI API does not retain them per their data policy. Transcripts and tasks live in your DB.
Why kanban + tasks instead of just kanban?
Kanban is great for triage and standups. Past 30 items, the wall of cards becomes noise. The Tasks list view is keyboard-first and dense, designed for getting through 50+ items. Same data, switch with one keystroke.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. The web app is a PWA, the inspector becomes a full-screen drawer below 960px, and the in-browser recorder uses MediaRecorder so you can capture meetings on iOS/Android without an extra app.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes. Pro and Team are month-to-month. Downgrade or cancel from settings, no email gauntlet. Self-host doesn't require any account.
