Getting Started is Simple

Set up your account with PocketID in under 2 minutes

1

Create Your PocketID Account

Visit pocketid.app and sign up for a free account. PocketID is your secure, passwordless authentication provider.

Important: Enable Passkey for Enhanced Security

After creating your PocketID account, go to Settings → Security → Add Passkey. This enables biometric authentication (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello) for secure, passwordless login.

2

Sign In to kAInban

Click "Sign in with PocketID" on the kAInban login page. You'll be redirected to PocketID to authenticate using your passkey or password.

3

Start Managing Tasks

That's it! You're ready to record meetings, transcribe audio, and let AI extract your tasks automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is PocketID and why do I need it?

PocketID is a secure, open-source identity provider that enables passwordless authentication using passkeys (biometric authentication like Face ID or Touch ID). It's more secure than traditional passwords and provides a seamless login experience.

Can I use kAInban without a PocketID account?

Yes! While we recommend PocketID for enhanced security, you can also create an account using email and password directly on kAInban.

How accurate is the AI transcription?

We use Azure OpenAI's Whisper model, which provides industry-leading accuracy (95%+) for transcriptions in multiple languages and accents.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We use industry-standard security practices and never share your data with third parties. Your recordings and transcriptions are stored securely and only accessible by you and your team.

Can I upgrade or downgrade my plan anytime?

Yes! You can change your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately, and downgrades will apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

Do you offer refunds?

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact us for a full refund.
