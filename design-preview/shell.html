Terminology & decision principles

The language reinforces the aesthetic. Workspace-OS verbs. Avoid ticketing-system nouns.

Use

  • Tasks — atomic units of work.
  • Projects — groups of tasks with a goal.
  • Inbox — AI's loading dock; unreviewed extractions.
  • Activity — recent events across workspace.
  • Actions — things the AI can do for you.
  • Workspaces — top-level tenant boundary.
  • Meetings — audio sources that produced tasks.

Avoid

  • Issues, Tickets — bug-tracker language.
  • Epics, Sprints, Stories — agile ceremony language.
  • Backlogs — implies dead work pile.
  • Cards as a user-facing noun (only internal).
  • Dashboard — implies admin panel.
  • Notifications use sparingly; prefer Activity.

Inspector vs Modal

  • Inspector — task detail, meeting detail, project detail. Context stays visible.
  • Modal — irreversible confirmation, multi-step wizard, auth flow. Demands full focus.
  • Popover — single-field picker (date, assignee). Inline.
  • Drawer (mobile) — inspector at full screen on phones.
